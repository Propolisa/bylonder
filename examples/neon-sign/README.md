# Neon Sign

Made in Blender 5.2 by `neon-sign.py` (`blender -b --factory-startup -P neon-sign.py`) and opened here with Blender's
own reader.

- **Words.** A text object, Open Late, with a little Extrude and a round Bevel, in a pink emissive material.
- **Frame and swoosh.** A Bézier circle scaled into an oval and a two point Bézier curve, both beveled into round
  tubes.
- **Wall.** A plane with a Brick Texture over its UVs into Base Color and, through Bump, Normal; two point lights in
  the tubes' colors light it.
- **Editing.** Tab on a text types into it; on a curve it shows the points and handles.

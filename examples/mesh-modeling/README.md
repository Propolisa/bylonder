# Rangefinder Camera

A rangefinder film camera on a desk, made in Blender 5.2 by `mesh-modeling.py` (`blender -b --factory-startup -P
mesh-modeling.py`) and opened here with Blender's own reader.

- **Body.** A box with loop cuts for the leather band, its top inset and extruded into the viewfinder housing, its
  edges beveled in three segments, and two windows inset into the front.
- **Lens and knobs.** Profiles spun 360 degrees: rings of quads, with ridges on the focus ring.
- **Edit it.** Select Camera Body and press Tab; 1, 2 and 3 pick vertices, edges or faces.

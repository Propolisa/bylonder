# Chess Endgame

Turned ivory and ebony chess pieces on a wooden board, lit by one low spot light, made by `chess.py` in Blender. Each
piece is a lathe of a profile; the board is one mesh of 64 squares with two materials. The pieces are small and close
together, a scene where Preferences > Navigation (Orbit Around Selection, Zoom to Mouse Position) and the keymap
change how it is to work in.

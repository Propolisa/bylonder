# Ceramic Owl

A glazed ceramic owl on a walnut plinth, made in Blender 5.2 by `sculpting.py` (`blender -b --factory-startup -P
sculpting.py`) and opened here with Blender's own reader.

- **Owl.** A quad sphere of about 10,000 faces, shaped by grab, draw, inflate and crease falloffs: ear tufts, a face
  of two discs, a beak, wings and rows of feathers. The eyes are separate spheres.
- **Sculpt it.** Select Owl and pick Sculpt Mode: V Draw, G Grab, C Clay Strips, Shift C Crease, I Inflate, S Smooth;
  Shift smooths with any brush, Ctrl inverts it, Ctrl Z undoes a stroke.

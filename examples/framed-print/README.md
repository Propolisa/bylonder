# Framed Print

Made in Blender 5.2 by `framed-print.py` (`blender -b --factory-startup -P framed-print.py`) and opened here with
Blender's own reader.

- **Print.** A sheet whose material reads Dusk Print, a screen print of mountains at dusk, as Base Color, and Paper
  Grain, a Non-Color height image repeated by a Mapping node, through Bump.
- **Frame and wall.** Black bars around a white mat, on a plaster wall under a spotlight.
- **Floor and bench.** Oak Boards, an image of boards each its own tone, repeated by a Mapping node.
- **Editing.** The Texture Paint workspace's Image Editor shows the images; the Shading workspace shows the Image
  Texture nodes.

# Window Light

A table by a window in late sun, made in Blender 5.2 by `rendering.py` (`blender -b --factory-startup -P
rendering.py`) and opened here with Blender's own reader.

- **Lights.** Sun through the window (Angle 1.5 degrees, Cast Shadow on) and Fill, a point light with a 0.8 m Radius.
- **World.** A dusk blue that lights the shadows and is what the copper and the glass reflect.
- **Materials.** Oak from a Wave Texture, plaster from a Noise Texture, glass from a Glass and a Transparent BSDF mixed
  by Fresnel, copper, a glaze and lemons.
- **Render.** Rendered shading in the 3D Viewport (Z > Rendered), or Render > Render Image through the camera.

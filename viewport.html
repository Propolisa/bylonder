Touch: tap to select; tap again to deselect. Double-tap to frame. Long-press for object actions. One finger orbits in Select mode or transforms in Move/Rotate/Scale mode. Two fingers pan and pinch to zoom. Multi enables additive selection. X/Y/Z constrain transforms; Snap and Fine work without a keyboard. Lift to commit, or use × to cancel an active operation.
G/R/S use screen-space movement and rotation; scale is radial from the selection pivot. X/Y/Z constrain world axes.
